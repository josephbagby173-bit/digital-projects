## Description
**Stop rebuilding the same session every song.**
Open the template, drop in your stems, and start mixing.

**What's included**
- 1 Ableton Live 12 Suite session (.als)
- 1 Quick Guide PDF (one page)

**Layout**
- **Drums group:** Kick, Snare, Toms, Overheads, Drum Bus
- **Bass group:** Bass DI, Bass Amp
- **Guitars group:** Rhythm Guitar L, Rhythm Guitar R, Lead Guitar, Clean Guitar
- **Vocals group:** Lead Vocal, Backing Vocals
- **3 return tracks:** Room Reverb, Plate Reverb, Delay
- 120 BPM (change to match your song)

**Built in**
- EQ Eight with role-based high-pass and tone settings on every track
- Compressor settings chosen per role
- Drum Bus track with Drum Buss for glue
- Limiter on the Main track

**Requirements**
- Ableton Live 12 Suite
- Uses only Live's built-in devices. No third-party plugins, no outside samples.

**Note:** this is a starting point, not a finished mix. Adjust levels, EQ and compression to your material.
