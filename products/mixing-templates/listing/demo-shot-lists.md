# Demo video shot lists (30-60 seconds each)

Rules for all three:
- Screen recording only (OBS or Windows Game Bar: Win+Alt+R). No voiceover needed; add one short text line per step in CapCut.
- Keep file paths, your email and account names off screen. Close other windows. Use stems you own.
- Record in Live's Arrangement or Session view with the track list visible.

## Hip-Hop / R&B (record this one first)
1. (0-5s) Open the template. Text: "Hip-Hop / R&B Vocal Mixing Template". Show the Beat and Vocals groups.
2. (5-15s) Drag your beat stems onto Kick, Snare / Clap, Hats, 808, Melody / Sample. Text: "Drop in your stems".
3. (15-25s) Drag a vocal onto Lead Vocal (and a double or ad-lib if you have one). Text: "Vocals are ready to go".
4. (25-40s) Press play. Let the 808 hit. Text: "808 ducks the melody".
5. (40-55s) Raise a send on the vocal to open the Vocal Plate / Slap Echo returns. Text: "4 returns built in".
6. (55-60s) End on the full track view. Text: "Live 12 Suite | stock devices only".

## Band / Guitar
1. (0-5s) Open the template. Text: "Band / Guitar Mixing Template". Show Drums, Bass, Guitars, Vocals.
2. (5-20s) Drag stems onto Kick, Snare, Overheads, Bass DI, Rhythm Guitar L/R, Lead Vocal. Text: "Drop in your stems".
3. (20-40s) Press play. Click the EQ Eight on one guitar track so the high-pass shows. Text: "EQ and compression set per track".
4. (40-55s) Raise a send to the Room or Plate Reverb. Text: "3 returns built in".
5. (55-60s) End on the full view. Text: "Live 12 Suite | stock devices only".

## EDM / Pop
1. (0-5s) Open the template. Text: "EDM / Pop Mixing Template". Show Drums, Bass, Synths, Vocals, FX.
2. (5-20s) Drag stems onto Kick, Clap / Snare, Sub Bass, Lead, Pad, Lead Vocal. Text: "Drop in your stems".
3. (20-40s) Press play through a bass + synth section. Text: "Kick ducks the Bass and Synths".
4. (40-55s) Raise a send to the Reverb or Parallel Compression return. Text: "3 returns built in".
5. (55-60s) End on the full view. Text: "Live 12 Suite | stock devices only".
