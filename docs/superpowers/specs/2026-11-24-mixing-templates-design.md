# Mixing Templates — Design Spec

Date: 2026-11-24
Subproject 3 of the digital-products plan (Etsy / Ko-fi / Gumroad).

## Goal

Produce three Ableton Live 12 Suite mixing templates that can be sold on Etsy, Ko-fi and Gumroad. The owner's overall goal is first income within 2 weeks to 1 month, so this subproject must be buildable and sellable without recording any guitar.

## Audience

Bedroom producers and new Ableton users. Templates are not genre-purist; they are practical starting points that are easy to understand.

## Deliverables

Three templates (`.als`):

1. **Band / guitar** — rock, metal, math-rock, emo. Drums, bass, guitars, vocals.
2. **Hip-hop / R&B vocals** — vocal chains, ad-lib buses, 808 and drum routing.
3. **EDM / pop** — kick/bass routing, synth buses, vocal chain, master chain.

For each template:
- A one-page PDF quick guide: routing, what each track does, where to drop audio.
- A short screen-recording demo (about 30-60 seconds): load the template, drop in audio, before/after on the owner's own stems. Previews only, not tutorials.
- Listing assets: cover image, preview images, description (formats, requirements, personal-use terms).

Plus a bundle listing of all three at a discount.

## Template contents

- Pre-routed tracks and group buses for the niche.
- Return tracks for reverb and delay.
- Master chain ending in a limiter.
- Starter processing per channel using normal device controls. No custom on/off switches (Live already provides device on/off).
- Color-coded groups and clear track names so new users can navigate.

## Constraints

- Live 12 Suite only. Stock Ableton devices only.
- No third-party plugins, no third-party or outside samples.
- Original work; no remakes of other artists' tracks.

## Build approach

1. Design each template's track list and signal chains (use Ableton's official manual for device behavior).
2. Build in the owner's open Live set through Producer Pal / AbletonMCP.
3. Save each clean session.

## Testing

- Owner loads each template fresh in Live 12 Suite and confirms nothing is missing or broken.
- Owner's own finished songs/stems serve as test audio and for before/after demos, so no new guitar recording is needed.
- Check: no missing-plugin or missing-file warnings on open.

## Selling

- Three separate listings plus one bundle, on all three platforms (Etsy, Ko-fi, Gumroad).
- Price below the $23.99 Etsy competitor (UltimateMixing, Live 12 mixing template, 392 reviews) to earn early reviews, then raise. Exact prices are set by the market research.
- Etsy listings must follow Etsy's digital-download rules.

## Out of scope

- Guitar-specific templates (existing `COOLGUITAR`, `DefaultGuitar`, `PolyphiaGuitar` etc.) — need a guitar signal to test; deferred.
- Full video tutorials.
- Standard / Intro editions of Live.
- Third-party plugin dependencies.

## Open items

- Final pricing (from market research).
- Exact track lists per template (settled during planning).
